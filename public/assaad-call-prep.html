For a standard Shopify store with Meta and Google — about 7 days. You share read-only access, we handle the rest. The Pilot plan onboarding is 7 days, Scale is 48 hours kickoff.

GA4 shows behavior — where users click. Shopify shows orders. Neither shows real profit after refunds, platform fees, and ad spend. That gap is what we reconcile.

Absolutely — that's exactly what I'd recommend. One client, one store, full pipeline. You see the value. Then we scale.
