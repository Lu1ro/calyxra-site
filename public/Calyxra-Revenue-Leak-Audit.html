01 — The Problem

The Revenue Gap Nobody Talks About

Ad platforms report every purchase event as revenue. But Shopify only deposits what's actually collected — after refunds, chargebacks, discounts, and failed payments are subtracted.

⚠️ The Result: Inflated ROAS
When you optimize campaigns based on reported ROAS, you're making decisions with phantom revenue — money that Meta says you earned but never actually hit your bank account. You could be scaling a campaign that's losing money while thinking it's profitable.
