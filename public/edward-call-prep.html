🆘 Tough Questions — Exact Answers

💬 "How do you pull the data?"

We use BigQuery as the warehouse. Shopify API for orders and payouts. Ad platform APIs for spend data. Then dbt as the transformation layer — it reconciles everything into standardized KPIs.

💬 "Littledata already sends data to BigQuery via GA4. How is this different?"

Exactly — that's the beauty of it. Your GA4 export lands in BigQuery with clean behavioral data. We add the financial layer on top — Shopify payouts, refunds, gateway fees, ad spend. The reconciliation happens IN BigQuery, using YOUR data as the foundation.
This is your strongest technical argument. His data + your transformation = full picture.

💬 "Is it self-serve?"

Not yet. We scope and deploy per client. Moving toward automation. But right now it's hands-on — which means the output is tailored to each agency's needs.

💬 "Who's using it?"

We're early stage — deploying on first live clients now. That's partly why I wanted to talk to you — to learn from someone who's been building in this space for 8 years.
Be honest. Oxford + Accenture background = he detects BS in 2 seconds. Honesty earns respect.

💬 "What's your pricing?"

For agencies, we have three tiers based on the number of stores — Pilot, Scale, and Pro. Plus a standalone Source-of-Truth Audit at €349. I can send you the full pricing page after the call.

💬 "How is this different from Triple Whale / Northbeam?"

They focus on attribution — which ad drove the sale. We focus on financial reconciliation. Meta says 50K. Shopify says 47K. We show why the gap exists — refunds, fees, gateway cuts, platform over-reporting. Different layer entirely. And ours is white-label for agencies.

💬 "Why should Littledata care?"

Your customers install Littledata. Tracking is fixed — GA4 is accurate. But their CFO opens Shopify, sees a different number than GA4, and the trust breaks again. That's not a tracking problem anymore — it's a reconciliation problem. And right now, nobody solves it automatically. We could be the answer Littledata recommends.

💬 "We already do this / we're building this"

Oh really? I'd love to hear how you approach it. Maybe there's overlap, maybe we're solving different parts. Either way — I'd learn a lot from hearing your perspective.
NOT defensive. Pure curiosity. If they have it — learn how. If they don't — they'll realize they need it.
